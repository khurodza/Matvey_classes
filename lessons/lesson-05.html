My favourite sportsperson is Oriane Bertone. She's a French rock climber and she lives in Paris. She's 164 cm tall. She's taller than other female climbers.

2

We use also and too to give extra information.

1. We use also the verb be.

2. We use also other verbs.

3. We use too at the of a sentence.

She is also one of the best. · She also eats healthy food. · She climbs with her brother, too.

Homework · Review · Comparatives

Compare two things

Writing · Checked

Write sentences using the comparative form of the adjectives.

Example football / tennis popular → Football is more popular than tennis.

1. zorbing / running exciting

2. water / fizzy drinks healthy

3. joggers / jeans comfortable

4. a table tennis ball / a tennis ball small

5. jumping / rock climbing easy

Short adjectives + -er (small → smaller, easy → easier, healthy → healthier). Long adjectives → more + adjective (more exciting). Don't forget than!
