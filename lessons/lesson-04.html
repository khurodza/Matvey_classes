Boy

Hey, Enes. You know that sport you do a lot?

Enes

Which one? I like lots of sport. Sailing, cycling …

Boy

No, I mean … er … is it goalball? I'm interested in finding out more about it.

Enes

Oh, goalball! Yeah! Well, that's my favourite sport. I play it every weekend I can.

Boy

Cool. There's an article here that says it's a Paralympic sport. Is that right?

Enes

Yes, it is. I think it's the most exciting sport in the world!

Boy

What is goalball like then? It's a team game. Is that right?

Enes

Yes. It's a lot of fun to play with other people. And it's a game that blind people, or people like me who find it difficult to see, can play. The sport uses a special ball which makes a sound. When I'm playing, I can hear where the ball is, so I know where it's going.

Boy

Is that difficult? I don't know if I could do that.

Enes

It's not so difficult. Like every sport, you have to practise.

Boy

And is the ball like a football or smaller than a football?

Enes

It's big, like a football, but it has three bells inside it, to make a noise.

Boy

Ah, so it's heavier.

Enes

Yes!

Boy

And how many people are in a team?

Enes

Each team has three players. To score a goal, you try to throw the ball across the goal line at the other end of the court. You can run, but you can't kick the ball. The people in the other team try to stop the ball with their bodies. They can stand up or get down on the floor. The game's really fast. It's faster than you think. The best players can throw the ball at 60 km per hour!

Boy

Wow, that's a lot! Where do you play?

Enes

We play at the sports centre. It's fun. Do you want to come and watch us next Saturday?

Boy

Sure. That's the most interesting plan for the weekend I can think of!
