Look at the table. Choose the correct words.

1. We use (there) for singular nouns and (there) for plural nouns in the past.

2. We use (there) was / were + not for .

3. The word order is in questions.

In questions the verb goes first: The water was clean. → Was the water clean? · There were wolves. → Were there any wolves?

Language in action · Questions

How to make questions with was / were

Look and learn

In a question, was / were jumps to the front — before the person or thing.

question word was / were who or what the rest

Yes / No questions

Statement Youwereat the zoo.
↓ swap them!
Question Wereyouat the zoo?
→ Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
Statement Therewasa baby seal.
↓ swap them!
Question Wastherea baby seal?
→ Yes, there was. / No, there wasn't.

Wh- questions

Put the question word in front of a yes / no question.

Yes / No …Wereyouat the zoo?
↓ add a question word
Wh- Wherewereyouyesterday?
How oldwasKai?
Whatwasyour favourite animal?
How many wolvesweretherein the park?
→ Answer with a full sentence: I was at home. · He was fifteen.
was → I / he / she / it / one thing · were → you / we / they / two or more. Look at the word after was / were to choose: Was Kai …? · Were the dolphins …?

A class discussion · Audio 7.01

Listen again and answer

Listening · Multiple Choice

Remember the Learn to learn tip: look for the question words and think about the answers before you listen. Then listen again and answer the questions.

Найди вопросительные слова и подумай об ответах до того, как начнёшь слушать. Потом послушай ещё раз и ответь на вопросы.

Example 1. How does one student describe wolves? — They are fast, dangerous and clever.

2. Why weren't there many wolves in Yellowstone Park for a long time?

3. How many wolves were in the park in the late 1990s?

4. Why was it difficult for the wolves at first?

5. Why is it difficult to see wolves in the park?

Show the audioscript
Teacher

Good morning, class. How are you today? Yesterday we talked about wolves. What do you remember about them?

Ari

They are fast and dangerous and clever like me.

Leo

OK, Ari, and there are lots of wolves living now in Yellowstone Park in the USA.

Teacher

Yes, that's right, Leo. But just a few years ago, that was not true. From the 1970s to the 1990s, scientists think that the number of wolves maybe went down to only a few in Yellowstone Park. Why do you think that was?

Leo

Because they went to a different place?

Ari

Because they didn't like the park?

Teacher

No, they didn't go to a different place, and they liked the park. Many years ago, a lot of wolves lived in Yellowstone Park, but in the early 1900s they started to disappear.

Leo

So why was that?

Teacher

Well, Leo, people killed the wolves. Farmers were scared of them, and sometimes the wolves attacked farm animals. People killed the wolves until 1935. After that, people thought it was the end of the wolf in Yellowstone. But, from 1995 to 1997, people moved a small group of about 40 wolves to Yellowstone.

Ari

Where were the wolves from? Were they from a zoo?

Teacher

No, Ari. They were wild wolves from different parts of the USA and from Canada. When the wolves began their new life in Yellowstone Park, it was really difficult for them. Sometimes there was no food in winter because it was really cold and they couldn't find any animals to eat.

Leo

How many wolves live there now?

Teacher

Scientists think there are more than 120 wolves in the park.

Ari

Is it easy to see the wolves?

Teacher

No. They are very careful and quiet. They hide from people.

Leo

That's not like you, Ari … haha!

Why → listen for because · How many → listen for a number · disappear = go away so that nobody can see it any more · hide = go where people can't see you.
