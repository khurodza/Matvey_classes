Amazing animals in danger

Listen & Read

Read and listen to the text and the fact files. Check your answers to step 1.

Amazing animals in danger

In the past, these animals weren't in danger and life wasn't the same. But things change and now people know about the problems and they are finding solutions.

Axolotl

In the past, there was a lot of water in Lake Xochimilco, Mexico. It was clean. There were a lot of axolotls in the water – about 6,000 axolotls per square kilometre. They were healthy and they weren't hungry. Now there are only 36 axolotls in the same area. Why? They're hungry because other fish eat their food. There's a lot of plastic in the water and it's dirty. But don't worry! Scientists and farmers are working to clean the water and help the axolotls.

Weight
56–225 grams
Habitat
Lake Xochimilco, Mexico City
Food
small fish, insects and worms
Amazing fact
It can grow new parts of its body.

Maned Wolf

In Brazil, there were almost 24,000 maned wolves in the past. Today, there are less than 15,000. What's the biggest problem? The wolves can't live in the forests because there aren't a lot of trees. But there are ways to fix this. There are now safe places in forests for wolves. Local people are learning about the wolves and helping to protect them.

Weight
20–25 kg
Habitat
Savannahs in Brazil and forests in South America
Food
small animals and insects, fruit and vegetables
Amazing fact
The maned wolf isn't really a wolf – it's a member of the dog family. It barks like a dog!

Mediterranean Monk Seal

Today, there are about 700 Mediterranean monk seals. How many monk seals were there in the past? 24,000. The ocean was their home and it was safe. Monk seals sometimes die because they swim into fishing nets. But there's good news! There are different nets now. The seals can swim in and out of them easily.

Weight
up to 315 kg
Habitat
caves and beaches of the Mediterranean Sea
Food
fish, octopus
Amazing fact
It can swim faster than a shark.
in danger = there are not many of them, and they can disappear · habitat = the place where an animal lives · solution = a way to fix a problem.

True / False / Doesn't say

2. The maned wolf is a member of the wolf family.

5. Pollution is a problem for all of these animals.

6. The maned wolf is the heaviest of the three animals.

7. People are finding solutions to help these animals.

DS = the text doesn't give this information. It isn't true or false — we just don't know from the text.

Polar bears are in town!

Reading · Writing

Read the article. What's special about the town of Churchill?

Polar bears are in town!
1

Churchill is a town on the Hudson Bay in Canada. Every October, November and December, it has special visitors: polar bears! They can't hunt seals until it is colder and there is more ice, so they visit the town looking for food.

2

In 2021, there were 870 people in Churchill. Around 12,000 tourists come every year. They come to see the polar bears – there are over 600 in the area and they often come into the town. When this happens, the police ask people to leave their cars open in the town. Then people can jump inside if they need to escape!

3

In 2023, polar bears came into Churchill earlier than usual. By the summer of that year, there were 76 phone calls to the police. To keep people safe – and the bears – wildlife experts took three of the animals to a place outside the town.

4

The worst year was 1983. On 29th November, a young male bear killed a man in Churchill. It is the only time this happened. But sometimes, bears can attack when they think people want to hurt them or their babies. In 2013, a bear attacked a man in the street. Experts moved it to outside the town. The bear was safe and the man was soon fine as well.

What's special about the town of Churchill?
