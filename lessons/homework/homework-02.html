Task 2 · Vocabulary · medium

Choose the correct verbs

Word Choice

Someone is describing a rugby match. Choose the correct verb in each pair.

Example 1. the players pass / score the ball to each other

I don't understand rugby! OK, the players pass the ball to each other. Then they up the field and put the ball over a line to points. They also the ball over a big goalpost. But sometimes the teams make a circle and push each other. Then one player the ball into the circle and another player from the same team gets it. And sometimes some players on top of other players!

Think about the meaning: the ball goes into the circle, so a player has to throw it there — and a player on top of another player is not jumping, the team lifts them up.

Task 4 · Grammar · medium

Comparatives: fill in the gaps

Fill in the Blanks

Complete the sentences with the comparative form of the adjective. The adjective is on the yellow label above each box.

Remember: short adjective → -er (fast → faster, big → bigger, easy → easier) · long adjective → more (more expensive) · irregular → good → better, bad → worse.
Example A tennis court is smaller (small) than a football pitch.

1. A football is big than a tennis ball.

2. Running is cheap than skiing.

3. For me, swimming is easy than surfing.

4. Basketball is exciting than golf.

5. My brother is good at chess than me.

6. Our team was bad last year than this year.

Task 5 · Grammar · challenge

Comparatives: find the mistakes

Fill in the Blanks

Four sentences have a mistake in the comparative. Write the correct form in the box. One sentence has no mistake — write OK for that one.

Example Football is popularer than hockey. → more popular

1. Tennis is more slower than table tennis.

2. A bike is more expensive than a ball.

3. My sister is taler than me.

4. Skiing is dangerouser than walking.

5. This ball is more heavy than that one.

Look for: more + -er together ✗ · -er on a long adjective ✗ · more with a short adjective ✗ · a missing double letter (big → bigger) ✗
