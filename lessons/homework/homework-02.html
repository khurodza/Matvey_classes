Task 2 · Vocabulary · medium

Choose the correct verbs

Word Choice

Someone is describing a rugby match. Choose the correct verb in each pair.

Example 1. the players pass / score the ball to each other

I don't understand rugby! OK, the players pass the ball to each other. Then they up the field and put the ball over a line to points. They also the ball over a big goalpost. But sometimes the teams make a circle and push each other. Then one player the ball into the circle and another player from the same team gets it. And sometimes some players on top of other players!

Think about the meaning: the ball goes into the circle, so a player has to throw it there — and a player on top of another player is not jumping, the team lifts them up.
