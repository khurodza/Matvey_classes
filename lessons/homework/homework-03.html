Tap an adjective to cross it out when you have used it.

The most + adjective The + double the final consonant + -est The + change y to i + -est The + adjective + -est The + irregular form
the most popular 2 the easiest 4 the best
1the most entertaining the biggest 3 the fastest 5
Look at the column heading — it tells you the rule. fit has one vowel + one consonant, so double the t. healthy ends in -y, so change it to -i. bad is irregular — it's not the baddest!
