Amazing animals: a day out and an article

Speaking: listen to Martha's story about a school trip to a safari park, learn how to ask about a day out (How was …? What was it like?) and how to react (No way! You're joking!). Writing: read about a llama that broke a world record, use on, in and ago with dates, and write your own article about a famous animal.

Multiple Choice

Meet Boateng de Oro

By Lara Wilson

1

Boateng de Oro is a clever llama. He lives on a farm in Italy with his owner, Walter Mair. Boateng and Walter take people into the Italian mountains. Boateng loves jumping.

2

In 2023, Walter and Boateng appeared on an Italian TV show. Lots of people watched the show. Boateng jumped 1.2 metres. He broke the world record. More than ten years ago, a llama called Caspa could jump 1.13m. On 22nd February, 2023, five days later, Boateng jumped 1.3 metres at home on the farm.

3

Now you know about clever llamas. Do you know any other animals with amazing abilities?

owner = хозяин · appeared on a TV show = was on TV · broke the world record = did it better than anyone before (побил мировой рекорд) · could = the past of can · abilities = things you can do
